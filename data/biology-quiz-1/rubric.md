# Biology Quiz 1 - Marking rubric

Each question is out of 10 and marked separately.

## Q1 Photosynthesis

### Inputs (3 points)
- 3: names carbon dioxide, water and light
- 2: names two of these
- 1: names one
- 0: none

### Outputs (2 points)
- 2: names glucose (or sugar) and oxygen
- 1: names only one
- 0: neither

### Location (2 points)
- 2: says it happens in chloroplasts and that chlorophyll absorbs light
- 1: only one of these
- 0: neither

### Energy conversion (3 points)
- 3: explains that light energy is converted into chemical energy stored in glucose
- 1: mentions energy only vaguely
- 0: no mention

## Q2 Cellular respiration

### Equation (3 points)
- 3: glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water (+ energy)
- 2: one substance missing or wrong
- 1: only some correct substances
- 0: none

### Location (2 points)
- 2: says it happens in the mitochondria
- 0: otherwise

### ATP (3 points)
- 3: says energy is released and stored as ATP
- 1: mentions energy without ATP
- 0: no mention

### Aerobic vs anaerobic (2 points)
- 2: contrasts aerobic (uses oxygen) with anaerobic (no oxygen, lactic acid or ethanol, less ATP)
- 1: describes only one
- 0: neither

## Q3 Water cycle

### Stages (4 points)
- 1 point each for evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection or runoff

### Energy source (2 points)
- 2: says heat from the sun drives evaporation
- 0: otherwise

### Example (2 points)
- 2: gives a relevant real-world example
- 1: vague example
- 0: none

### Transpiration (2 points)
- 2: explains that plants release water vapour through their leaves
- 0: not mentioned
