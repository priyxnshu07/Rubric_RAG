Biology Quiz 1 - Marking rubric (each question is out of 10 points and marked separately)

Q1 Photosynthesis - Inputs (3 points): 3 = names carbon dioxide, water and light; 2 = names two of these; 1 = names one; 0 = none.
Q1 Photosynthesis - Outputs (2 points): 2 = names glucose (or sugar) and oxygen; 1 = names only one; 0 = neither.
Q1 Photosynthesis - Location (2 points): 2 = says it happens in chloroplasts and that chlorophyll absorbs light; 1 = only one of these; 0 = neither.
Q1 Photosynthesis - Energy conversion (3 points): 3 = explains that light energy is converted into chemical energy stored in glucose; 1 = mentions energy only vaguely; 0 = no mention.

Q2 Cellular respiration - Equation (3 points): 3 = glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water (+ energy); 2 = one substance missing or wrong; 1 = only some correct substances; 0 = none.
Q2 Cellular respiration - Location (2 points): 2 = says it happens in the mitochondria; 0 = otherwise.
Q2 Cellular respiration - ATP (3 points): 3 = says energy is released and stored as ATP; 1 = mentions energy without ATP; 0 = no mention.
Q2 Cellular respiration - Aerobic vs anaerobic (2 points): 2 = contrasts aerobic (uses oxygen) with anaerobic (no oxygen, lactic acid or ethanol, less ATP); 1 = describes only one; 0 = neither.

Q3 Water cycle - Stages (4 points): 1 point each for evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection or runoff.
Q3 Water cycle - Energy source (2 points): 2 = says heat from the sun drives evaporation; 0 = otherwise.
Q3 Water cycle - Example (2 points): 2 = gives a relevant real-world example; 1 = vague example; 0 = none.
Q3 Water cycle - Transpiration (2 points): 2 = explains that plants release water vapour through their leaves; 0 = not mentioned.
