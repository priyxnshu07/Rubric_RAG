# Public speaking: course notes

## Grab attention with a hook

The first sentence of a speech is the hook. Good hooks are a question ("Have you ever wondered...?"),
a surprising fact, or a very short story. A hook makes the audience want to keep listening.

## One clear main message

A good speech has one main message. Say it early, in one simple sentence, so the audience knows
what you want them to think. Everything else in the speech should support that message.

## Reasons and examples

Give reasons for your opinion. Each reason is stronger with an example, a fact or something that
happened to you. Two or three reasons are enough for a short speech.

## End strongly

Finish by repeating your main message in a few words, then give a call to action: ask your audience
to do something, like "Join the club" or "Try it this week". Ending with only "thank you" feels flat.

## Speaking tips

Practise out loud, speak slowly, look at your audience and pause after important points.
